## Route-map action vocabulary

- `KEEP` — current route already represents a strong workspace boundary.
- `REDESIGN` — keep route boundary; redesign presentation later.
- `MOVE` — relocate existing capability under a clearer workspace.
- `MERGE` — combine navigation concepts without duplicating underlying business logic.
- `SPLIT` — replace a tab/pseudo-route or mixed page with focused deep-linkable destinations later.
- `CONTEXTUALIZE` — keep capability accessible through related tasks rather than primary navigation.
- `HIDE` — remove from primary navigation while preserving supported deep-link access when appropriate.
