## Research
- MDN preconnect guidance: `rel="preconnect"` can warm DNS/TCP/TLS for critical cross-origin resources.
- MDN DNS prefetch guidance: use `dns-prefetch` as a lower-cost fallback and avoid excessive preconnects.
- MDN preload guidance: font preloads require CORS matching and should be used selectively because excessive preloads can compete with other critical resources.

## Decision
W12-02 is closed for this measured slice. Further bundle/resource optimization remains a separate task and must begin from a fresh build-manifest/resource-artifact comparison rather than speculative refactoring.
